      Girl Scout Gold Award

      I built a patient-records and visit-tracking system for community health workers at the Power of Love Foundation in Matero, a settlement in Lusaka, Zambia. It is in daily use supporting more than 620 children and adults.

      • Serves an HIV care program of 550+ children and a mental health program of ~70 more
      • Named one of 50 Girl Scout Emerging Leaders in San Diego
      • Keynote speaker, Girl Scout Silver Award Ceremony, 2026
